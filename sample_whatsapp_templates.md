# WhatsApp Message Templates

This document contains sample message formats you can use when creating new WhatsApp message templates in the **Add New Template** section.

## Fields Overview

When adding a new template, you will use the following fields:
* **Template Name**: A short, recognizable name for the template (e.g., `Order Confirmation`).
* **Template Message**: The actual message content that will be sent to the customer. You can use placeholders like `[Customer Name]` or `[Order Number]`.
* **Preview Message**: A visual preview of how the message will look when formatted.

---

## Sample Templates

### 1. Sales Follow-up
**Template Name:** `Sales Follow-up`
**Template Message:**
```text
Hello [Customer Name],

Thank you for visiting [Shop Name] recently! We noticed you were interested in our new collection. 

If you need any help finding the right product or have any questions, feel free to reply to this message. We'd be happy to assist you!

Best regards,
[Shop Name] Team
```

### 2. Order Confirmation
**Template Name:** `Order Confirmation`
**Template Message:**
```text
Hi [Customer Name]! 🎉

Great news! Your order #[Order Number] has been successfully confirmed and is currently being processed by our team. 

We will notify you again as soon as your order has been shipped. 

Thank you for shopping with [Shop Name]!
```

### 3. Transaction Receipt
**Template Name:** `Transaction Receipt`
**Template Message:**
```text
Dear [Customer Name],

We have received your payment of [Amount] for invoice #[Invoice Number]. Thank you for your prompt payment!

Your transaction was successful. If you have any questions regarding your billing, please don't hesitate to reach out.

Regards,
[Shop Name] Billing Team
```

### 4. Discount Voucher
**Template Name:** `Discount Voucher`
**Template Message:**
```text
Hello [Customer Name]! 🎁

As a thank you for being a loyal customer, we are giving you a special [Discount %] OFF voucher!

Use promo code: *[Promo Code]* at checkout on your next purchase to claim your discount.

Shop now: [Website Link]
(Valid until [Expiry Date])
```

### 5. Delivery Update
**Template Name:** `Delivery Update`
**Template Message:**
```text
Hi [Customer Name],

Good news! Your order #[Order Number] is out for delivery today. 🚚

Our courier will be arriving soon. Please make sure someone is available to receive the package.

Track your package here: [Tracking Link]

Thank you!
```
